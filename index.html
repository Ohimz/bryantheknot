OUR STORY

Our Journey Together

Every love story is unique. Ours is a beautiful testimony of God's perfect timing, unwavering faithfulness, and enduring love.

Built on Faith. Guided by Love.

People always ask us how we met, and we’re pretty sure it was fate doing what it does best. What started as two people getting to know each other back in 2020 when the world was on pause, quickly turned into nonstop conversations, we shared plenty of laughs, and realizing we genuinely enjoyed each other’s company. Before we knew it, one date turned into I want to be with this person a while longer, and with the grace of God we’ve been together with each other ever since! Through all the adventures, inside jokes, and countless memories over the past six years, we’ve built a relationship filled with love, faith, patience, and a whole lot of laughter. Now we’re excited to make it official and can’t wait to celebrate this next chapter with the people we love most.

Through every season, God continued to lead our steps, strengthening our relationship and reminding us that His plans are always perfect.

Today, with grateful hearts, we look forward to beginning this new chapter together as husband and wife. We are honoured to celebrate this special day with our family and friends.
